# Itinerary PDF Studio

The studio turns an itinerary and its quotation into a polished A4 PDF you can send to the customer. It is usually 5 to 7 pages: cover, overview, the day-wise plan (three days per page), the price, and the policies.

## Open the studio

Open an itinerary, choose **Package studio**, then the **5. Itinerary PDF** tab. You can also use the **Itinerary PDF** button on the itinerary page.

## What you choose

- **Quotation and price option.** The option marked as selected is chosen for you. Pick another if you want to show a different price.
- **Theme.** Corporate Navy, Ocean Teal, Charcoal Minimal, Modern Bold or Emerald Executive.
- **Cover photo.** Choose a photo from your library, or leave it on Automatic to use the cover photo from the Photos tab.
- **Sections.** Turn Overview, Hotels, Day plan, Quotation, Inclusions and exclusions, Cancellation policy, Terms and Bank details on or off. A turned-off section is left out of the PDF.

The preview beside the controls updates as you choose. On a phone, use the **Edit** and **Preview** tabs and the bar at the bottom. **Save settings** keeps your choices for next time.

## Where the content comes from

Everything is read live, so if you change an activity on the itinerary or a price line on the quotation, the studio shows it straight away. Dates for each day are the trip start date plus the day number. The cancellation policy comes from the quotation, then the package, then your agency default.

Your internal cost prices and markup are never read and never appear in the PDF.

## Download and share

- **Download PDF** opens the file; the name is the quotation number followed by `-itinerary.pdf`.
- **Share on WhatsApp** saves a copy under **Documents** and opens WhatsApp with a message and a link to the PDF. The link works for 7 days. If the customer has no WhatsApp or phone number saved, you are given the link instead.

:::requires documents.upload
Saving a copy and sharing needs permission to upload documents.
:::

## Good to know

- Amounts show as `INR 42,500` because the PDF fonts have no rupee sign.
- A trip longer than nine days adds more day pages.
- The preview is close to the PDF, but very long text can continue onto an extra page in the file.
