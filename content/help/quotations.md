# Quotations

A quotation turns an itinerary and your costs into a price the customer can accept.

![The quotations list: 1 search, 2 status filter, 3 templates, 4 new quotation, 5 a quotation's status (its total is on the right)](/guide-img/quotations.png)

## Creating one

1. Open **Quotations > New quotation** and choose the customer and, if you have one, the lead and itinerary.
2. Add line items with quantity and price. Taxes are calculated for you.
3. Pick a **template** for the terms, cancellation and payment wording, or write them by hand.
4. Download the **PDF** and send it from **Communications**.

## Costs and profit

Cost price and profit are only shown to people whose role includes them. Salespeople can be allowed to see prices without seeing your margin.

## Templates

**Quotations > Templates** keeps reusable wording so every quotation says the same thing. Change a template and only new quotations change; sent ones stay as they were.

> A sent quotation is a record of what the customer was told. If the price changes, prepare a new quotation so the history stays accurate.
