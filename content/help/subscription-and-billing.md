# Your plan, usage and billing

![Plan and billing: 1 usage against your limits, 2 the plans to compare, 3 payment history](/guide-img/billing.png)

New agencies start with a free trial. Its end date is shown at the top of the page.

## Usage and limits

**Settings > Plan and billing** shows your plan and how much of each limit you have used: active staff, branches, bookings this month, report exports this month, AI requests and document storage. When a limit is reached, only creating _new_ records of that kind is blocked. Nothing already stored is ever deleted.

## Changing plan

Choose another plan to see a preview first. If you use more than the new plan includes, the preview lists exactly what, and the change still keeps all your data. A downgrade starts at the end of the current billing period; an upgrade starts when the payment is confirmed. Changing plan needs a recent password check.

## If a payment fails

1. **Past due**: the payment provider retries. Everything keeps working.
2. **Grace period**: everything still works while you fix the payment, and reminders are sent to people who manage billing.
3. **Suspended**: your account becomes **read-only**. You can view and download everything, but not create new records. A successful payment restores full access straight away.

Nothing is deleted at any step.

## Payment history and receipts

Every successful payment is listed with a numbered **receipt** you can download as PDF.

> A receipt confirms a payment. It is not a GST tax invoice.

:::requires settings.manage
Online billing needs the platform's payment provider to be configured by the system operator. If **Choose plan** says billing is not configured, contact support.
:::
