# Notifications and job orders

![Notifications: 1 unread only, 2 mark all as read, 3 preferences, 4 the By email column](/guide-img/notifications.png)

## Notifications

The bell shows what needs you: enquiries assigned to you, new bookings, payments received or overdue, invoices issued, approaching departures, visa document problems, job deadlines and team changes. Open one to go straight to the record. Opening it never gives you access you do not already have.

The **Notification preferences** below the list let you choose, for each kind, whether you want it in the app, by email or both. Emails go out once a day with the daily check and contain a headline and a link only, never customer or passport details.

## Job orders

A **job order** is a piece of work with an owner and a deadline, such as "collect passports for the Dubai group". Create one under **Job orders**, assign it, and the person can accept it, comment and move it to done. Every change is recorded in its history.

:::requires jobs.assign
You can assign jobs to anyone who is active. If a person already holds as many open jobs as their limit allows, the assignment is refused until work is finished or the limit is raised under **Settings > Roles and access**.
:::

Jobs that pass their deadline show as overdue and notify the assignee and assigner.
