# Getting started

Smart Travel CRM keeps your enquiries, customers, quotations, bookings, visas and payments in one place. Everything belongs to your agency only: other agencies cannot see your records and you cannot see theirs.

## The screen

![The dashboard with the numbered areas: 1 menu, 2 search, 3 quick add, 4 notifications, 5 help, 6 your profile](/guide-img/dashboard.png)

1. **Menu** on the left. You only see the sections your role allows. On a phone it opens from the top-left button.
2. **Search** at the top finds customers, bookings and visas you are allowed to see. Type at least two letters and press Enter.
3. **Quick add** creates a lead, customer, quotation and more without leaving the page.
4. **Notifications** (the bell) show assignments, payments, departures and other events. You choose which ones reach you under Notifications.
5. **Help** (the question mark) opens this Help center.
6. **Your profile** holds your own time zone, date format and two-step verification.

## First steps

1. Open the **Help center** and follow the _Getting started_ checklist. It ticks itself off as you add real records.
2. Invite your team under **Settings > Team** and choose a role for each person.
3. Add a customer and record an enquiry, then build an itinerary and send a quotation.

:::requires settings.manage

## For administrators

Check **Settings > Regional settings** first so dates and times show in your agency's time zone, then **Settings > Branding** for your logo and PDF look. Turn on two-step verification for owners and admins under **Settings > Security**.
:::

> You only see what your role allows. If a menu item or button is missing, ask an administrator; do not look for a workaround.
