# Leads and customers

A **lead** is an enquiry that may become a booking. A **customer** is the person or company you sell to. One customer can have many leads, quotations and bookings.

![The leads list: 1 search, 2 stage filter, 3 list or board view, 4 new lead, 5 the stage of a lead](/guide-img/leads.png)

## Recording an enquiry

1. Open **Leads** and choose **New lead** (or use Quick add).
2. Pick or add the customer, then enter the trip details, dates, budget and where the enquiry came from.
3. Assign it to a team member. They receive a notification.

Every lead gets a permanent **reference number** such as LD-2026-000001 the moment it is saved. It never changes and is never given to another lead, even if a lead is deleted. Enter the lead's full name, **mobile number with country code** (for example +91 98765 43210), email and nationality. The nationality box is searchable: type a few letters. Mobile numbers are saved exactly as typed, so a leading zero is kept.

On the leads list you can search by name, reference number, mobile number or email, and filter by status, destination, source, assigned staff and the date the lead was created.

## Enquiries and history

A customer may ask for more than one trip. Open a lead and use its **Enquiry History** tab to add each enquiry; every enquiry gets its own number such as ENQ-2026-000001. You can link a quotation or itinerary to the enquiry it answers. The other tabs show the lead's bookings, itineraries, quotations and follow-ups, and **Activity Timeline** lists everything that happened, newest first, with who did it and when. If nothing has happened yet a tab says so; the CRM never makes up history. You only see the tabs your role allows.

## Moving a lead along

A lead moves through these statuses: New, Contacted, Requirement collected, Quote prepared, Quote sent, Follow-up, Negotiation, Confirmed and Lost. Change the status on the lead page, or switch to the **Board** view to see every stage side by side; every change is recorded with who made it and when.

## Follow-ups and notes

On the lead page use **Add follow-up** to set a task with a date, and **Add note** for anything the next person should know. Open follow-ups appear under **Follow-ups** in the menu, together with any **next follow-up date** you set on a lead. The number beside Follow-ups is how many are due today or overdue.

## Customers

Open **Customers** to search, view history and add new people. A customer page shows their leads, quotations, bookings and payments together.

:::requires settings.manage

## For administrators

If people only see their own records, that is a **record scope** set on their role under **Settings > Roles and access**. Leads, customers, quotations, bookings, invoices and tasks can be limited to own, assigned, branch or all records.
:::

> Duplicate customers are the most common mess. Search before you add.
