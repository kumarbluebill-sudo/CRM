# Leads and customers

A **lead** is an enquiry that may become a booking. A **customer** is the person or company you sell to. One customer can have many leads, quotations and bookings.

![The leads list: 1 search, 2 stage filter, 3 list or board view, 4 new lead, 5 the stage of a lead](/guide-img/leads.png)

## Recording an enquiry

1. Open **Leads** and choose **New lead** (or use Quick add).
2. Pick or add the customer, then enter the trip details, dates, budget and where the enquiry came from.
3. Assign it to a team member. They receive a notification.

## Moving a lead along

A lead moves through these statuses: New, Contacted, Requirement collected, Quote prepared, Quote sent, Follow-up, Negotiation, Confirmed and Lost. Change the status on the lead page, or switch to the **Board** view to see every stage side by side; every change is recorded with who made it and when.

## Follow-ups and notes

On the lead page use **Add follow-up** to set a task with a date, and **Add note** for anything the next person should know. Open follow-ups appear under **Tasks**.

## Customers

Open **Customers** to search, view history and add new people. A customer page shows their leads, quotations, bookings and payments together.

:::requires settings.manage

## For administrators

If people only see their own records, that is a **record scope** set on their role under **Settings > Roles and access**. Leads, customers, quotations, bookings, invoices and tasks can be limited to own, assigned, branch or all records.
:::

> Duplicate customers are the most common mess. Search before you add.
