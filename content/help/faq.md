# Frequently asked questions

## Can other agencies see my data?

No. Every record belongs to one agency and the database itself prevents anyone from reading another agency's records.

## Who in my agency can see what?

Only what their role allows. Owners and admins can review roles, and the audit log records changes.

## Does the CRM send messages by itself?

No. Automatic reminders only prepare drafts. A person reviews and sends them.

## Does it delete anything when my plan ends or a payment fails?

No. The account can become read-only, and you can still view and download everything.

## Is the AI assistant always right?

No. It can help draft text and summarise, but check anything that matters. It has daily limits per plan, and it only works with records you are allowed to see.

## How do I get my data out?

Reports can be exported as CSV (subject to your plan's monthly allowance and a recent password check). Owners can also download encrypted backups.

## Are the invoices legal tax invoices?

The software helps prepare GST invoices, but your accountant should confirm your setup before you rely on them. It does not file returns.

## Can I change a date format or time zone?

Yes, under **Profile** for yourself, and under **Settings > Regional settings** for the agency.

## Where do I report a problem?

Tell your administrator with the page, what you did, the message and the time.
