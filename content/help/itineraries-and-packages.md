# Itineraries and tour packages

An **itinerary** is the day-by-day plan for a trip. A **package** is an itinerary dressed up for customers with photos, pricing and terms.

![The itineraries list: 1 search, 2 templates, 3 import, 4 new itinerary, 5 an itinerary](/guide-img/itineraries.png)

## Building one

1. Open **Itineraries** and choose **New itinerary**.
2. Add days and the activities, hotels and transfers for each day. Times for flights and other events can have their own time zone, so a Dubai departure shows Dubai time first.
3. Use **Preview** to see what the customer will see.

## Importing an existing plan

Choose **Import** and upload a document. The tool reads it and proposes a draft; **always check the draft** before using it, because importing is a convenience and not a guarantee.

## Packages

Open an itinerary and choose **Package** to set the theme, photos, pricing, policies and call to action. Packages can be saved as templates and copied for the next customer.

## Status

Itineraries are Draft, Published or Archived. Archiving keeps the itinerary; nothing is deleted.
