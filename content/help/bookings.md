# Bookings and operations

A booking is a confirmed trip. It holds the travellers, the services you bought from suppliers, documents and payments.

![The bookings list: 1 search, 2 status filter, 3 approved quotations (where a booking starts), 4 a booking's status (its total and balance are on the right)](/guide-img/bookings.png)

## Creating a booking

Create it from an accepted quotation so prices carry over, or start from **Bookings**. Add each traveller, and each service (hotel, flight, transfer, activity) with its supplier and dates.

## Services and time zones

A flight or check-in has its own local time and zone. The page shows the event's local time first and your own time next when they differ, so nobody has to calculate.

## Suppliers and vouchers

Suppliers live under **Suppliers**. For each service you can create a **voucher** PDF to send to the supplier or traveller.

## Documents

Passports, tickets and other files are stored privately and only opened through your signed-in account. Sensitive passenger details are visible only to roles that include that permission.

## Payments

The booking page shows what has been paid and what is due. See _Payments and invoices_.

> A departure date is a calendar date and never shifts with time zones. Times of day do.
