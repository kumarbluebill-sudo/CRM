# Staff, roles and access

![Roles and access: 1 search staff, 2 role, 3 branch, 4 open job limit, 5 which records a role can see, 6 what a role can do](/guide-img/roles.png)

## Inviting people

**Settings > Team** lists your team and invitations. Choose a role for each invitee. A deactivated person cannot use the CRM and does not count toward your plan's staff limit.

## Roles

Seven built-in roles cover most agencies: Owner, Admin, Sales manager, Sales executive, Operations, Accountant and Viewer. Under **Settings > Roles and access** you can also create **custom roles** and tick exactly the permissions they need, grouped by area (leads, bookings, payments, visa and so on).

You cannot give a role a permission you do not hold yourself, and you cannot change a role above your own. Role changes need a recent password check and are recorded in the audit log.

## Branches

Branches group staff by office and give each its own time zone. Create them under **Settings > Regional settings** and place people in a branch on the roles page.

## Record scope

For each role you can choose how much of the leads, customers, quotations, bookings, invoices and tasks it sees: **own** records, **assigned** to them, their **branch**, or **all**. The default is all.

## Workload limits

The **Open job limit** column on the roles page sets the most active jobs a person may hold at once.

> Permissions decide what someone can do. Hiding a menu item is only a convenience; the database enforces the real rules.
