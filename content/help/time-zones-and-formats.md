# Time zones and date formats

![Regional settings: 1 default zone, 2 date format, 3 time format, 4 branches](/guide-img/regional.png)

## How it works

Every moment is stored once, in universal time, and shown in the right zone for the reader. Changing a zone never moves a booking or reminder; it only changes how it is displayed. Daylight-saving changes are handled for you.

Your own display zone is chosen in this order: your **profile** setting, then your **branch**, then the **agency** default.

## Your own settings

Open **Profile** to choose your time zone, date format (for example 31/12/2026 or 12/31/2026), 12 or 24 hour clock and first day of the week.

:::requires settings.manage

## Agency settings

**Settings > Regional settings** sets the agency default and lets you add branches with their own zones. The agency zone also decides where "today" starts in dashboards, reports and reminders.
:::

## Flights and appointments

Events such as a flight have their own local time. A Dubai departure shows Dubai time first and your time next, so a team in another country reads it correctly.

> A departure **date** has no time zone and never shifts. Only times of day do.
