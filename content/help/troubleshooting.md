# Troubleshooting

## "You don't have permission to do that"

Your role does not include that action. Ask an administrator to check your role under **Settings > Roles and access**. If you can see only some records, your role may be limited to your own, assigned or branch records.

## I cannot create anything new

Your plan limit may have been reached, or your agency may be in a read-only state after a failed payment. A message on screen says which. See _Your plan, usage and billing_. Existing records stay viewable.

## I was asked for my password again

That is a safety check for sensitive actions. Enter your password and you return to where you were.

## I was signed out

Sessions end after a long idle period. Sign in again. If you are blocked after wrong passwords, wait a few minutes.

## I lost my authenticator

Ask an owner or administrator to help you. Do not try to bypass two-step verification.

## Times look wrong

Check your own time zone under **Profile**, then your branch and agency settings. See _Time zones and date formats_. Stored times are never changed by these settings.

## A page says Page not found

You may follow a link to a record you cannot see, or a section your role does not include. Go back and use the menu.

## A backup failed

Open **Settings > Backups** and read the message. Your data is unaffected. Tell your administrator, who checks the destination and key.

## Still stuck

Note what you were doing, the exact message and the time, and send it to your administrator.
