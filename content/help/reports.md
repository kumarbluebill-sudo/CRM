# Reports and exports

![The reports page: 1 date range and Apply, 2 quick ranges, 3 CSV downloads, 4 the sales pipeline](/guide-img/reports.png)

## Reading a report

Pick a date range at the top, or use a quick range such as Last 30 days. Day boundaries follow your agency time zone, so "today" means today where you work. Figures are limited to the records your role may see.

## Exporting

Under **Download CSV** you can export **Bookings**, **Payments** or **Leads** for the chosen dates. Exports need a recent password check, are written to the audit log, and count against your plan's monthly export allowance. When the allowance is used up the page says so and offers an upgrade.

> Treat exported files like the customer data they contain. Store them securely and delete them when you are done.

:::requires billing.manage
Your plan and current export usage are under **Settings > Plan and billing**.
:::
