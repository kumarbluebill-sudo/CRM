# Visa management

![The visa dashboard: 1 my open applications, 2 documents to review, 3 new enquiry, 4 new application, 5 applications by status](/guide-img/visa.png)

## From enquiry to application

1. **Visa > Enquiries > New** records what the customer needs: country, visa type and travellers.
2. **Convert** the enquiry to an **application**. The required-documents checklist is generated from the visa product you chose.
3. Add each traveller and upload their documents. Each document is reviewed and either approved or rejected with a reason.
4. Move the application through its statuses until it is closed.

## My visa work

**My visa work** lists the applications assigned to you. Missing or rejected documents raise notifications for the person responsible.

## Products and settings

:::requires visa.price.edit
Countries, visa types, document types and prices are set under **Visa > Products** and **Visa settings**. Prices and supplier costs are visible only to roles that include them.
:::

## Importing data

**Import visa data** loads records from a CSV file. It checks the file first and only then asks you to confirm; rows that already exist are skipped and nothing is overwritten.
