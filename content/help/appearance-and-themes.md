# Appearance and themes

## Choosing your own look

Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Appearance** (or go to **Your profile > Appearance and theme**). Pick one of the eight themes:

- **Professional Blue**, **Modern Purple**, **Travel Teal**, **Forest Green** and **Sunset Orange** use a coloured accent on clean neutral surfaces.
- **Midnight Dark** has dark backgrounds with light text and is always dark.
- **Minimal Light** uses white surfaces and quiet grey accents.
- **Custom Theme** lets you choose your own colours.

Then choose a mode: **Light**, **Dark** or **System default**, which follows your device. The preview on the page shows your choices straight away. Nothing else changes until you choose **Apply theme**. **Cancel** discards your changes.

Your choice is saved to your account, so it stays after you refresh or sign in again, on any device. It never changes anyone else's screen.

## Your own colours

Changing any colour turns the theme into your Custom Theme, starting from the colours you were looking at. You can set the primary and accent colours, the sidebar background and text, the active menu colours, the page, card and border colours, the button colour, and the text and muted text colours. Light and dark have their own colours, so use the **Light colours / Dark colours** switch.

The page warns you when text would be hard to read. You cannot apply a theme whose main text, muted text, sidebar text or active menu text does not have enough contrast with its background. Colours must be written like `#1d4ed8`.

## Agency default

:::requires settings.manage
Under **Settings > Appearance** an administrator can choose the default look for everyone in the agency. People who have not chosen their own theme see the agency theme. A person can switch back with **Follow my agency's theme** or **Reset to agency default**. **Reset to default** on the agency page removes the agency theme and brings back the standard blue look.
:::

Nothing is changed until someone chooses a theme, so a new agency sees the standard look.

## The sidebar

The menu is grouped into **Main menu**, **Sales & travel**, **Operations** and **Management**, followed by Visa, Communications and More. You only see the pages your role allows. The number beside **Follow-ups** is how many are due today or overdue, and the number beside **Notifications** is how many are unread.

Use the arrow at the top of the sidebar to collapse it to icons; it remembers your choice. On a phone or tablet, open the menu with the button at the top left. Choose a page, press the close button, tap outside the menu or press Escape to close it. Your profile, Appearance and Sign out are at the bottom.

> Agency colours used on quotations, invoices and the customer page are set under **Settings > Agency branding** and are separate from the theme you see in the CRM.
