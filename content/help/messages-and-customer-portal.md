# Messages and the customer portal

![All messages: 1 status filters, 2 templates and automation, 3 the message list](/guide-img/communications.png)

## Messages

**Communications** keeps every message in one outbox. Use templates for common wording. Email can be sent from the CRM when email is set up; WhatsApp messages are prepared for you to send.

Automatic reminders (for example a payment reminder) only create **drafts**. A person reads and sends them; nothing is sent by itself.

## The customer portal

From a booking you can create a private link for the customer. They can see their trip, pay online where your agency has enabled it, and send you a request, all without an account. The link is secret: anyone who has it can open that page, so send it only to the customer.

:::requires portal.manage
Links can be revoked at any time from the booking. Revoke a link immediately if it was sent to the wrong person.
:::

> Never paste a portal link into a public place.
